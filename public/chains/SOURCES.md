# Chain logos

Self-hosted so the bridge never depends on a third-party image host.
Testnets use the logo of their parent network.

- Most SVG logos: [web3icons](https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons) (MIT License), "background" variant.
- `edge.webp`, `morph.webp`, `pharos.webp`: [DefiLlama icons](https://github.com/llamao/icons), resized to 160 px.
- `arc.svg`: Arc mark from [arc.network](https://arc.network), placed on a round navy background.

All marks belong to their respective projects and are used only to identify the networks.
